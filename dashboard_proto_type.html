          Connection

          In the AYCD Inbox app, open Settings, then Tasks (API), and copy your API key. Keep Inbox open while we check out for you, since the key only works while the app is running.

          Gmail needs an app password, not your normal password. Create one in your Google Account under Security, 2-Step Verification, App passwords.

          iCloud needs an app-specific password, not your Apple Account password. Create one at account.apple.com under Sign-In and Security, App-Specific Passwords. Two-factor authentication has to be on.

          Your key or app password is only kept for this visit and is never saved in your browser.
